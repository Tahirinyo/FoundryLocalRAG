The Cedar Grove greenhouse opens to visitors at 9:00 a.m. and closes at 4:30 p.m. from Tuesday through Saturday. It is closed on Sundays and Mondays.

The north growing room is reserved for tomatoes and peppers. The south growing room is used for herbs, including basil, mint, and thyme.

Visitors may take photographs from the marked walkway, but they must not touch plants or enter a growing room without a staff escort.
